## How Old Navy Super Cash works

Super Cash is one of Old Navy’s more predictable savings systems, but it is easy to misunderstand because earning and redeeming happen at different times.

The official Super Cash page says shoppers can earn one code after a qualifying purchase of **$25 or more**. During a redemption period, the code can provide:

- **$20 off** a purchase of $50 to $99.99
- **$40 off** a purchase of $100 to $149.99
- **$60 off** a purchase of $150 or more

Online shoppers receive the code by email, generally within 24 hours. Encore members can also find saved offers in their account. In-store shoppers may receive a paper code at checkout.

The practical detail is the redemption threshold. If your cart is sitting at $48, adding a $3 item may unlock the $20 discount, assuming the item qualifies and the promotion is active. That can be worthwhile. Adding $20 of merchandise just to reach a higher Super Cash tier usually deserves more thought.

Super Cash also has restrictions:

- It is available only during specific redemption periods.
- It cannot be used at other Gap Inc. brands.
- It is not valid at clearance centers.
- It generally cannot be combined with another coupon or promotion.
- Gift cards and third-party merchandise may be excluded.
- A returned or cancelled order may affect eligibility.

The official terms also say that Encore members may apply reward points alongside Super Cash. That makes membership more useful when you already have points available, but it does not turn every promotional combination into a stackable discount.

### When Super Cash is actually useful
